# Corner Cafe - proposed Privacy Policy addition (DRAFT FOR REVIEW)

Proposed section: Cookies and advertising measurement

Our website uses essential technical functions to help you browse and submit enquiries or cake orders. These essential functions are needed for the website to operate.

If you choose Accept marketing in our cookie preferences, we may use Meta Pixel to understand how visitors interact with our website after seeing promotions on Facebook or Instagram. This may include visits to pages, cake selections, the start of an order and successful order enquiries. Meta may use cookies or similar technology for advertising measurement and attribution. Meta processes information under its own privacy terms.

You can choose Reject optional and continue browsing or placing a cake order. Marketing tracking should not start unless you have accepted it. You may change your choice later through the Cookie settings control on our website. Where technically appropriate, withdrawing consent will prevent further optional Meta tracking and remove applicable first-party marketing cookies.

We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, allergy information or cake messages to Meta as event details. Our operational order records remain separate from marketing event measurements.

For privacy-related enquiries, contact cornercafe@jacksfiji.com.

REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION: Verify all third-party trackers, actual data recipients, cookie lifetimes, international transfers, statutory notice/consent requirements in applicable jurisdictions, accuracy of claims about stored identifiers and deletion, and alignment with the company's approved privacy policy. Legal/privacy owner must approve.
