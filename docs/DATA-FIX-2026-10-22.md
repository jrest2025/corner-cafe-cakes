  "Father's cake Day-2026", which was never synced. The register had 21 cakes on those two days; the sheet lists about 65.
- 43 cakes not already in the register (checked by phone and name against the online and walk-in sheets) were added
  through the sheet-sync path: `registerRow('walkin', …)` from `sheet-sync/register.mjs`, then
  `register_ingest('walkin', rows, false)` (add only; nothing existing changed or marked missing).
  Keys `walkin|row9001` to `walkin|row9043` (`register.rec_key` = `sheet:walkin|row9001` …). Each row's note says where it came from.
- Order (booked) date: not on that sheet, so left empty. These cakes count in "cakes due" (green) only, never in
  "booked" (purple). Nothing was guessed.
- Two second cakes on their own sheet lines (same customer, same cake) were held as possible duplicates and confirmed
  "not duplicate" in `reg_decisions`.
- Checked in the same transaction: the 566 records counted before stayed exactly the same.
- Result: cakes due 5 Sep 20 (was 8), 6 Sep 44 (was 13). Keys recorded in `_fix_20261022_fathers_day`.
