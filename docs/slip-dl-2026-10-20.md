# Kitchen slip on DL paper, 110 × 220 mm (20 October 2026)

Roneel: "the slip has to dl because we will use Dl sized paper for print". The café prints kitchen slips on DL paper,
110 × 220 mm. DL had been set up as 99 × 210 mm, and the live paper setting was A5.

Status: candidate, not published. Needs Roneel's approval before any of the three release steps below.

## What changes

| Where the slip comes out | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emailed slip (send-emails function, `slip_pdf.js`) | DL page 99 × 210 mm | DL page 110 × 220 mm |
| Download slip (PDF), staff app | DL page 99 × 210 mm | DL page 110 × 220 mm |
| Print on this device, staff app | `@page` 99 mm × 210 mm, slip fitted to 87 × 196 mm | `@page` 110 mm × 220 mm, 6 mm margins, slip fitted to 98 × 206 mm |
| Settings, Kitchen printing | "DL (99 x 210 mm)" | "DL (110 x 220 mm)" |
| Paper setting (`app_config.kitchen_slip_paper`) | A5 | DL |

The slip starts at a larger type size on DL (about 11% larger than the old DL) and only steps down when an order has a
lot of text, so it always fits on one page: steps 0.96, 0.87, 0.78, 0.69, 0.62 (were 0.86, 0.78, 0.70, 0.62). A5 is
unchanged and stays available in Settings.

## Release steps (only after approval, all three together)

1. Deploy `send-emails` (version 23 → 24) with the new `supabase/functions/send-emails/slip_pdf.js`; the other three
   files are unchanged; `verify_jwt` stays off.
2. Staff page footer loader → `build/staff_slip_dl.py` output (`dist/app/staff-slip-dl-manifest.json`), then publish.
3. Paper setting to DL through `admin_print_settings_save` (keeps the printer addresses; the printer check resets, as it
   does whenever the paper changes). Then send a test slip from Settings, Kitchen printing, and press It printed correctly.
