## What changes

| Where the slip comes out | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emailed slip (send-emails function, `slip_pdf.js`) | DL page 99 × 210 mm | DL page 110 × 220 mm |
| Download slip (PDF), staff app | DL page 99 × 210 mm | DL page 110 × 220 mm |
| Print on this device, staff app | `@page` 99 mm × 210 mm, slip fitted to 87 × 196 mm | `@page` 110 mm × 220 mm, 6 mm margins, slip fitted to 98 × 206 mm |
| Settings, Kitchen printing | "DL (99 x 210 mm)" | "DL (110 x 220 mm)" |
| Paper setting (`app_config.kitchen_slip_paper`) | A5 | DL |

The slip starts at a larger type size on DL (about 11% larger than the old DL) and only steps down when an order has a
lot of text, so it always fits on one page: steps 0.96, 0.87, 0.78, 0.69, 0.62 (were 0.86, 0.78, 0.70, 0.62). A5 is
unchanged and stays available in Settings.
