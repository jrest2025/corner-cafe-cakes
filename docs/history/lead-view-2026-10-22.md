# Booking time view on Analytics (22 October 2026)

An alternative view beside the protected Daily cake demand chart. A "View" switch above the chart offers
"Booked and due" (the existing chart, default) and "By booking time" (new). Each device remembers the choice.

- By booking time: one bar per pickup day (cakes due). The bar is split by how far ahead the cakes were booked:
  8 or more days, 3 to 7 days, 1 to 2 days, same day, booking date not known. Tapping a day lists its cakes and holidays.
- Summary for the chosen period (last 30 days, last 90 days, this year, all time), busiest day outlined in amber,
  Fiji public holidays shaded (from the protected chart's holiday list), "Orders taken today" shown separately.
- Counting matches the protected chart: cancelled, declined and not confirmed orders are left out; an order date is
  used only when the register marks it reliable (odok) and it is on or before the pickup date.
- The protected chart code and calculations are not changed. The new view only hides it (display) while chosen.
