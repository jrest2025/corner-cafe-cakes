# Easier staff forms (20 October 2026)

Published to cornercafefiji.com and www.cornercafefiji.com on 20 October 2026, about 11:19 AM Fiji. Approved by Roneel
("deploy").

## What changed

Layout and order only. The same fields, rules, checks and saving; nothing in the database changes.

- New order, tablets: the form gets most of the width and the slip preview beside it is narrower. Step labels stay whole
  words (no "Custome / r").
- New order, each step: the required questions come first.
  - Customer: mobile, name, marketing permission, order source and order date, then the optional email and "How did they
    hear about us?".
  - Make it yours: allergies and occasion (both required) first, then inscription, candles, cake requirements and notes.
    The inscription stays in view because it is used on most orders (88%; candles 32%).
  - Cake requirements (eggless, gluten-free, dairy-free, vegan; about 5% of orders) sit behind one tap until one is chosen.
  - The step no longer puts the cursor in the inscription box on arrival, so the step title stays in view.
- Finish: payment first, then a compact summary, then Save.
- "Start over" moved to the top of the step, away from Back and Next. It still asks "Clear this order?" before clearing.
  Where "View slip" is shown (phones, upright tablets), Start over sits on the "Step x of 5" line, left of it.
- Add customer: name, mobile, best way to reach them and marketing permission first; other number, email, company,
  address, birthday, preferences and notes behind "More details" (shown straight away when any of them is filled). The
  corporate customer tick is a tap-sized switch.
- Change order: opens with every section closed, so "Who asked" and "Reason" sit next to Save until a section is opened.
