Festive FAQs

Do you offer custom festive cakes?

Selected cakes can be customised with advance notice. Final options depend on the festive production schedule.

Can I place a bulk or corporate order?

Yes. Corporate and group orders are welcome, and quantities can be discussed directly with the team.

Do you deliver?

Collection from Corner Café is preferred. Delivery can be considered for selected larger orders once confirmed with the team.

How far in advance should I order?

Early ordering is recommended, particularly for the week leading into Christmas when production capacity is limited.
